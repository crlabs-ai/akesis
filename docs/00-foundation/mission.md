# Product Mission: Akesis

## 1. Operational Mission Statement
Akesis exists to eliminate the friction of software pipeline failures by providing deterministic, observable, and safe AI-assisted remediation.

## 2. What Akesis Does for Its Users
For software engineers, DevOps teams, and engineering leaders, Akesis:
1.  **Listens:** Asynchronously intercepts failed CI/CD workflow runs across repositories.
2.  **Isolates:** Parses voluminous, unstructured logs to pinpoint the exact root cause, failed file, and line numbers.
3.  **Synthesizes:** Constructs minimal, standards-compliant unified diffs targeted precisely at the failure.
4.  **Validates:** Compiles and tests proposed fixes in isolated, ephemeral sandbox environments.
5.  **Delivers:** Opens clean, evidence-backed Pull Requests requiring a single human approval to merge.

## 3. Guiding Mandate
Every feature, interface, and prompt in Akesis must directly serve one goal: **reducing the mean time to repair (MTTR) of software delivery pipelines while maintaining absolute safety and developer trust.**
